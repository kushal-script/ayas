# Domain grounding and severity rules for steel and metal surface inspection before coating (plates, sheets, fabricated parts), with Indian standards and industry context, for a citeable demo rules engine

(Research, 14 Nov 2026. Every finding carries its source; UNVERIFIED and (calc) are marked.)

## Summary

BOTTOM LINE: The plate standards (EN 10163-2:2004 and its identical ISO twin, ISO 7788:2021) make three calls in the same order a rules engine would. (1) Every crack, shell and seam is a defect, whatever its size. (2) Other discontinuities count as acceptable "imperfections" or repairable "defects" depending on DEPTH relative to nominal thickness, plus the share of the inspected surface they affect (2 / 5 / 15 %). (3) The defect is then repaired by grinding within a stated allowance or by welding, depending on subclass. For a 3 mm plate (3 <= t < 8 mm) the published limits are 0.2 mm (imperfection), 0.4 mm (left unrepaired only if the affected area is <= 5 %) and a 0.3 mm grinding allowance. The free public versions of these tables are the iTeh previews and IACS Rec. 47, so the numbers can be cited.
KEY GAP: a single 2D camera at about 0.065 mm/px measures length, width, area, count, cluster spacing and distance to an edge. It does not measure depth. So the engine should (a) grade by size, area fraction and location, which can be measured; (b) send any depth-governed decision to HOLD for an operator check with a gauge or the team's dye-penetrant kit (ISO 7788 itself names dye penetrant as the way to show a defect has been removed); (c) never auto-accept a crack.
Rust and surface preparation: ISO 8501-1 rust grades A-D are defined by mill-scale state and visible pitting, not by percentages. India adopts them through IS 9954:1981 (Amendment 1 of 2010 points to ISO 8501-1:2007). The camera can give an "estimated rust grade (visual analogue)", plus a rust-area % on bands borrowed from ISO 4628-3 (0.05 / 0.5 / 1 / 8 / 40-50 %). ISO 8501-3 P1-P3 covers spatter, pits, edges and laminations, but the camera can mostly only flag "P3 not met" (feature present). Adhesion and edge radius need a person.
Indian clauses: IS 2062 cl. 7 allows grinding down to 4 % below the minimum specified thickness, 4-7 % by agreement, and weld repair only if the thickness after removing the defect is not reduced by more than 20 %. IS 2062:2011 was withdrawn after 29 Jun 2026 and replaced by IS 2062 (Part 1):2025. The BIS change list does not name cl. 7, so the wording is probably unchanged (UNVERIFIED). IS 1079 and IS 513 only say "free from harmful defects", with acceptance agreed between buyer and supplier. That is a good argument for a severity scheme the team defines itself.
Lighting: the human visual-testing norm (ISO 17637) asks for at least 350 lx at the surface, 500 lx recommended. The device should make its own LED light dominate and report the ambient range it was tested in, rather than claim "any lighting".
Material mismatch: the owned coupons are aluminium, and aluminium does not form red rust or mill scale. Buy a few mild-steel (IS 2062 E250) 3 mm coupons for the rust and pitting classes. UNVERIFIED that the team has any yet.
Indian context: crude steel output was 170.15 Mt in FY2025-26 (Ministry of Steel, Jul 2026), and India is the 2nd largest producer. Secondary producers including MSMEs hold 47 % of crude-steel capacity (2024-25). Peenya (Bengaluru) became a Special Investment Region in Jun 2025, with about 13,000 MSMEs reported, though counts vary between sources. Published human-inspection error rates for complex tasks are 20-30 % (Sandia / Drury). No India-specific inspection-error statistic was found, so word that carefully.
DEMO table: recommendations 1-7. All thresholds there are ILLUSTRATIVE unless marked as a standard value.

## Findings

- EN 10163-2:2004 definitions: 'imperfections' are surface discontinuities other than cracks, shell and seams whose depth and/or area are at or below a specified limit. 'Defects' exceed that limit, and all cracks, shell and seams are defects regardless of depth or area. Scope: hot-rolled plates and wide flats, 3 mm <= t <= 400 mm. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/19739/f5d288708c5a448c92c214085433b89d/SIST-EN-10163-2-2005.pdf)
- EN 10163-2 classes: in Class A the remaining thickness under discontinuities or ground areas may fall below the tolerance-standard minimum. In Class B it may not. Subclass 1: grind or chip then weld, permitted. Subclass 2: welding only if agreed at order. Subclass 3: no weld repair. Default if nothing is specified: Class A, subclass 1. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/19739/f5d288708c5a448c92c214085433b89d/SIST-EN-10163-2-2005.pdf)
- EN 10163-2 Table 1, maximum permissible depth of imperfections by nominal thickness t: 3-<8 mm 0.2 mm; 8-<25 mm 0.3; 25-<40 mm 0.4; 40-<80 mm 0.5; 80-<250 mm 0.7; 250-400 mm 1.3. Any number of these is allowed. Remaining thickness below the minimum is allowed on at most 15 % of the inspected surface. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/19739/f5d288708c5a448c92c214085433b89d/SIST-EN-10163-2-2005.pdf)
- EN 10163-2 Table 2, maximum depth of discontinuities that may be left unrepaired when their summed affected area is <= 5 % of the inspected surface: 3-<8 mm 0.4 mm; 8-<25 mm 0.5; 25-<40 mm 0.6; 40-<80 mm 0.8; 80-<150 mm 0.9; 150-<250 mm 1.2; 250-400 mm 1.5. Area with remaining thickness below the minimum is limited to 2 %. Deeper than Table 2, or more than 5 % of the area, must be repaired. Cracks, shell and seams must always be repaired. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/19739/f5d288708c5a448c92c214085433b89d/SIST-EN-10163-2-2005.pdf)
- EN 10163-2 Table 3 (Class A grinding, up to 15 % of the inspected area): permitted grinding depth below the minimum thickness is 0.3 mm for 3-<8 mm, 0.4 for 8-<15, 0.5 for 15-<25, 0.6 for 25-<40, 0.7 for 40-<60, 0.8 for 60-<80, 1.0 for 80-<150, 1.2 for 150-<250 and 1.4 for 250-400 mm. The free preview stops before Table 4 and the welding clause 6.3.3. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/19739/f5d288708c5a448c92c214085433b89d/SIST-EN-10163-2-2005.pdf)
- ISO 7788:2021 copies the same class A/B scheme, subclasses and Tables 1-3. It defines the discontinuity vocabulary: rolled-in scale and pitting, indentations and roll marks, scratches and grooves, spills and slivers, blisters, hot tears, sand patch, crack, shell and seams. Cracks, shell and seams are always defects. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/73845/2d335b97dbeb42f798b5604f5c4a6676/ISO-7788-2021.pdf)
- ISO 7788:2021 clause 6.1: depth is measured from the product surface, after the discontinuity has been ground out. Affected area is the discontinuity plus a 20 mm margin, or a rectangle 20 mm from its edges. Discontinuities closer than 40 mm count as one cluster. A defect must be ground out to full depth with a smooth transition, and in a dispute complete removal may be shown by magnetic particle or dye penetrant testing. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/73845/2d335b97dbeb42f798b5604f5c4a6676/ISO-7788-2021.pdf)
- On a 100x100 mm coupon (10,000 mm2), one isolated 2 mm pit has an ISO 7788 affected area of (2+40)x(2+40) = 1,764 mm2, which is 17.6 % of the coupon. So the standard's 5 % / 15 % area rules are dominated by the 20 mm margin on demo-sized coupons. (calc) (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/73845/2d335b97dbeb42f798b5604f5c4a6676/ISO-7788-2021.pdf)
- SCI guidance note GN 3.05 (steelconstruction.info): material delivered from the mill still carries mill scale, and a pre-fabrication blast will usually reveal any unacceptable discontinuities. For bridge steelwork, class A3 (plates) and C3 (sections) with no weld repair are generally treated as adequate, and are required by the UK Specification for Highway Works cl. 1805.3.3. (https://steelconstruction.info/images/d/db/GN-3-05.pdf)
- IACS Rec. 47 Rev.10 (2021), shipbuilding quality standard, free PDF. Minor imperfections are pitting, rolled-in scale, indentations, roll marks, scratches and grooves. Defects are cracks, shells, sand patches, sharp-edged seams, and minor imperfections beyond Table 1. Table 1 depth limits for t < 20 mm: 0.2 mm at 15-20 % affected area, 0.4 mm at 5-15 %, 0.5 mm at 0-5 %, with the affected area drawn 20 mm around the discontinuity. Loose weld spatter must be removed. (https://iacs.s3.af-south-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/20095943/rec-47-rev10-sep-2021-ul.pdf)
- IACS Rec. 47 cl. 6.7: pits shallower than 1/3 of the original plate thickness may be filled with coating or pit filler. Deeper pits or grooves are welded flush. Plates should be renewed if deep pits are clustered or remaining thickness is below 6 mm (ship context). For a 3 mm plate, 1/3 t = 1.0 mm. (calc) (https://iacs.s3.af-south-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/20095943/rec-47-rev10-sep-2021-ul.pdf)
- IS 2062:2011 cl. 7: finished steel shall be reasonably free from surface flaws, laminations, rough or jagged edges and other harmful defects. Minor surface defects may be ground out if local thickness is not reduced more than 4 % below the minimum specified thickness, or 4-7 % by mutual agreement. Weld repair, by agreement, only if after removing the defect the thickness is nowhere reduced more than 20 %. No weld repair for E250C, E275C and E300-E650. (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S10/is.2062.2011.pdf)
- For a 3 mm IS 2062 plate the clause 7 limits work out to about 0.12 mm (4 %), 0.21 mm (7 %) and 0.6 mm (20 %). The 4 % and 7 % figures are measured from the minimum specified thickness; nominal thickness was used here as an approximation. (calc) (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S10/is.2062.2011.pdf)
- BIS circular of 10 Apr 2026: IS 2062:2011 is replaced by IS 2062 (Part 1):2025 'Structural Steel Part 1 Hot Rolled Medium and High Tensile Steel', and the old standard stands withdrawn after 29 Jun 2026. The circular's list of significant changes does not mention clause 7 (freedom from defects). UNVERIFIED that the 4 % / 7 % / 20 % wording is unchanged in the 2025 text. (https://www.services.bis.gov.in/tmp/Circular_XkUR_2026-04-16.pdf)
- IS 1079:2009 (hot-rolled carbon steel sheet and strip) cl. 12: cut lengths must be free from harmful defects that affect end use. Coils are expected to carry more surface defects, and acceptance standards for them may be agreed between buyer and supplier. When mill edges are specified, edge defects must lie within 5 mm of the coil edges. (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S10/is.1079.2009.pdf)
- IS 513:2008 (cold-reduced sheet): surfaces for unexposed parts may have pores, slight pitting, small markings, scratches and light discoloration, while exposed parts must be reasonably free of them. Only one side is inspected unless agreed. Clause 9.1 requires freedom from scale, rust, blisters, lamination, pitting, porosity, and cracked or torn edges. UNVERIFIED whether IS 513 (Part 1):2016 is the current edition. (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S10/is.513.2008.pdf)
- IS 9954:1981 (reaffirmed 2000) rust grades. A: fully covered by adherent mill scale, little rust visible. B: rusting has begun and mill scale has begun to flake. C: mill scale rusted away or easily scraped, little pitting visible. D: mill scale rusted away, considerable pitting visible. Preparation grades are St 2 / St 3 for scraping and wire-brushing, and Sa 1 / Sa 2 / Sa 2½ / Sa 3 for blasting. Designations combine the two, e.g. 'B Sa 2½'. Amendment 1 (Aug 2010) refers to ISO 8501-1:2007. (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.9954.1981.pdf)
- IS 9954 procedure: compare the steel with the pictorial prints, holding the book with light coming towards the viewer. This is a visual, lighting-dependent comparison, not a measurement. (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.9954.1981.pdf)
- ISO 8501-1 summary from SCI: Sa 1 light, Sa 2 thorough, Sa 2½ very thorough, Sa 3 'visually clean steel'. Bridge specifications often require Sa 2½ or Sa 3. Sharp cut edges may need grinding to about a 2 mm radius. (https://steelconstruction.info/Surface_preparation)
- ISO 8501-1:2007 is still the published edition. A revision (ISO/DIS 8501-1, ISO project 85360) was in enquiry with voting closing around Mar 2026. UNVERIFIED whether a new edition has been published since. (https://genorma.com/en/standards/pren-iso-8501-1-rev)
- ISO 8501-3:2006 sets preparation grades P1 (light), P2 (thorough) and P3 (very thorough) for welds, edges and other areas with imperfections, which may show before or after blast cleaning. Its introduction notes that such imperfections are usual starting points for corrosion. A 2025 edition exists; check which edition is cited. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/22525/dad96ebf938048ac8779d7f28d762d21/SIST-EN-ISO-8501-3-2007.pdf)
- ISO 8501-3 Table 1 per a secondary summary. Weld spatter: P1 remove loose, P2 remove loose and lightly adhering, P3 none. Pits and craters: P1/P2 open enough for paint to penetrate, P3 none. Laminations, shells and slivers: P1 remove lifted material, P2/P3 none visible. Cut edges: P3 radius >= 2 mm. Indentations and roll marks: P2 smoothed, P3 none. Secondary source; the Table 1 text is not in the free preview. (https://graco.com/gb/en/products/surface-preparation/how-to/detect-defects-in-steel-surfaces.html)
- Indian railway practice: a 2026 tender's special conditions require grit blasting to Sa 2½ ('near white metallic') per IS 9954-1981 / IRS-B-1-2001, and at least one coating layer applied within 4 hours of blasting (metallising context). (https://mail.bbjconst.com/tendermar26/Addl_Spl_Condition.pdf)
- IS 1477 (Part 1):1971 (painting ferrous metals, pretreatment) says even minute traces of oil and grease must be removed before any paint is applied, using solvent or alkaline degreasers. The 2000 revision is the current reference per the IS 9954 amendment, but the 1971 text was the one read. (https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.1477.1.1971.pdf)
- ISO 8502-4 (condensation) sets a default of the steel surface at least 3 °C above dew point before painting, unless agreed otherwise. ISO 8502-3 grades dust by tape test with quantity ratings and size classes, but the acceptance limit comes from the project specification. UNVERIFIED that 'rating <= 2' is a common default. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/15718/cd49173442e64b538396bf8f7cf74919/ISO-8502-4-1993.pdf)
- ISO 4628-3:2016 (rusting of painted surfaces) degrees and approximate rusted area: Ri 0 = 0 %, Ri 1 = 0.05 %, Ri 2 = 0.5 %, Ri 3 = 1 %, Ri 4 = 8 %, Ri 5 = 40-50 %. This scale is for coated steel, so using it as an area-severity proxy on bare steel is a borrowing. ISO 4628-3:2024 adds a percentage-band method. (https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66400/a99d9afe610643adaa5aca79729a4177/ISO-4628-3-2016.pdf)

Through unofficial copies: shallow imperfections may be ground to sound metal within the minimum thickness, removal below the minimum is cause for rejection, and weld repair needs purchaser approval. The specific '7 % / 1/8 in' grinding limit is UNVERIFIED. The current edition is A6/A6M-26. (https://store.astm.org/a0006_a0006m-26.html)

## Recommendations

- DEMO TABLE, row 1, CRACK (incl. linear indications). Measure: length L in mm from the mask. Minor: none. Major: none. Critical: ANY L. Disposition: REJECT, then quarantine and QA. Action text: 'Crack-like indication. Stop, tag the part, do not grind or paint over it. Confirm with red-dye penetrant and refer to QA.' Basis: EN 10163-2 §6.2.1.2.3 and ISO 7788 §3.10 / §6.3.1 (cracks are always defects; dye penetrant confirms removal); ZMicro App. A (cracks not allowed). Never auto-accept a crack, even at low confidence.
- DEMO TABLE, row 2, SCRATCH. Measure: width W and length L in mm. ILLUSTRATIVE thresholds. Minor: W < 0.2 mm and L < 25 mm, so ACCEPT and log. Major: W 0.2-0.5 mm, or L >= 25 mm, or 3 or more scratches within a 40 mm cluster, so REWORK: blend smooth and re-inspect. Critical: W > 0.5 mm, or the scratch reaches an edge or hole, or bare metal shows through a coating, so HOLD for QA with a depth check. Action text: 'Check depth with fingernail or gauge. On 3-8 mm plate, up to 0.2 mm may be left; 0.2-0.4 mm only if the affected area is <= 5 %; deeper must be repaired.' Basis: EN 10163-2 / ISO 7788 Tables 1-2 and §3.5; ZMicro widths as a cosmetic reference.
- DEMO TABLE, row 3, DENT / INDENTATION. Measure: equivalent diameter D in mm. ILLUSTRATIVE thresholds. Minor: D < 4 mm, ACCEPT. Major: 4 <= D < 6.5 mm, HOLD for depth check with straightedge and feeler gauge; accept if depth <= 0.4 mm on 3 mm plate (Table 2 analogue), otherwise rework or straighten. Critical: D >= 6.5 mm, or within 5 mm of an edge, hole or bend, REJECT and send to QA (fit and function). Basis: ZMicro (3.8 / 6.35 mm diameter, 1.27 mm depth); ISO 7788 §3.4.
- DEMO TABLE, row 4, PITTING. Measure: per-pit diameter d, count n, defect-area fraction f, and clustering (edges < 40 mm apart). ILLUSTRATIVE thresholds. Minor: isolated pits, d < 0.5 mm, n <= 5, f < 0.5 %, ACCEPT. Major: a cluster, or f 0.5-5 %, or d 0.5-2 mm, REWORK by blasting or grinding to sound metal, then check pits are open for paint (ISO 8501-3 P2) or fill them. Critical: f > 5 %, or d > 2 mm, or pits together with rust (rust grade D), HOLD or REJECT and measure pit depth and remaining thickness. Basis: EN 10163-2 §6.2.1.1.2 (5 % rule); ISO 7788 §6.1.2 (40 mm cluster); IACS Rec. 47 §6.7 (shallow < 1/3 t).
- DEMO TABLE, row 5, RUST / CORROSION. Measure: rust-coloured area fraction R (% of inspected surface) plus whether pits are present, giving an 'estimated ISO 8501-1 / IS 9954 grade A-D (visual analogue)'. ILLUSTRATIVE thresholds. Minor: R <= 1 % (spot rust), CLEAN locally with power tool to St 3 and re-inspect. Major: 1 % < R <= 8 %, or estimated grade B/C, full surface prep to Sa 2½ before painting. Critical: R > 8 %, or estimated grade D (rust with visible pitting), HOLD for a thickness and pit-depth check, possible reject. Basis: IS 9954 / ISO 8501-1 definitions; ISO 4628-3 Ri 3 (1 %) and Ri 4 (8 %) borrowed as band edges; Indian railway Sa 2½ practice. Use steel coupons, because aluminium does not show red rust.
- DEMO TABLE, row 6, CONTAMINATION (oil/grease, water, dust). Measure: area fraction and type. Oil or grease, any amount: MAJOR, so CLEAN by solvent or alkaline degreasing before any blasting or painting, then re-inspect (IS 1477-1 §6.1). Water: MAJOR, so HOLD painting; dry the part and confirm the surface is at least 3 °C above dew point (ISO 8502-4). Light dust: MINOR, blow or vacuum, and tape-test per ISO 8502-3 if painting is next. Contamination alone never leads to REJECT; the disposition is always CLEAN and RE-INSPECT. Training seed: GC10-DET oil-spot and water-spot classes.
- DEMO TABLE, row 7, MODIFIERS. Severity goes up one level if the finding is within 5 mm of an edge, hole, weld or bend (ILLUSTRATIVE; the IS 1079 5 mm edge band is only an analogy), or if the count or cluster rule triggers. It may go down one level on surfaces marked hidden or non-cosmetic (ZMicro class C/D analogue), but never for cracks. Confidence is a separate gate: below threshold, status becomes 'UNCERTAIN, operator to confirm', whatever the severity. Every log row stores class, mm measurements, px/mm calibration, location, severity, rule ID, standard reference string, confidence, the action shown, and the operator override.
- Use the standards' own three tiers as the UI vocabulary: 'Imperfection, acceptable (log)' / 'Defect, repair (clean, blend or grind, then re-inspect)' / 'Critical defect, reject or hold for QA'. Map them to Minor / Major / Critical in the report. This matches EN 10163 / ISO 7788 terms and the organisers' rule that severity is team-defined.
- Handle depth explicitly. The camera reports L, W, area, count, cluster and edge distance only. Any rule that depends on depth (scratch, dent, pit) emits 'depth not measured, operator check' and goes to HOLD rather than ACCEPT when above the Minor size band. Put the published depth numbers (0.2 / 0.4 mm for 3-8 mm plate; IS 2062 4 % / 7 % / 20 %) into the advisor's action text as targets for the operator's gauge or penetrant check.
- Llama advisor: pass the structured JSON (class, mm sizes, location, severity, rule ID, reference string) and have it fill fixed action templates. Do not let it invent standard numbers. Put a whitelist of reference strings, such as 'EN 10163-2 Tbl 1 (3-8 mm: 0.2 mm)' and 'IS 2062 cl.7.2 (4 %)', in the system prompt, and check the output against that list.
- Pick 5 classes for Phase 1: crack, scratch, dent, pitting, rust. Add 'contamination (oil/water)' only if time allows. Map them to dataset classes: NEU-DET scratches, pitted surface and crazing (crack-like); GC10-DET oil and water spots. Add the team's own steel and aluminium coupon images, captured with the device's LEDs.
- Lighting claim: say the device supplies its own controlled illumination at the surface, targeting the >= 500 lx visual-testing level of ISO 17637. Report the ambient range actually tested (dark room, office, window or sunlight) along with precision, recall and F1 for each condition. Do not claim 'works in any lighting'. For rust colour, include a grey or white reference patch in the frame so the colour can be normalised (own suggestion, UNVERIFIED for this hardware).
- Wording to USE: 'rule-based severity informed by EN 10163-2 / ISO 7788, ISO 8501-1 / IS 9954, ISO 8501-3 and IS 2062 cl. 7'; 'thresholds illustrative for demo, to be agreed with the customer per IS 1079 / IS 2062 mutual-agreement clauses'; 'estimated rust grade (visual analogue)'; 'pre-coating surface inspection, decision support'; 'operator confirms depth and cracks with gauge or dye penetrant'.
- Wording to AVOID: 'compliant with', 'certified to' or 'BIS/ISO approved'; 'measures defect depth'; 'detects all cracks'; 'replaces NDT or inspectors'; 'zero defects'; 'works in any lighting' (without stating the tested range); 'ISO grade Sa 2½ achieved' (the camera cannot certify cleanliness); treating confidence as severity; precise Peenya unit counts without a qualifier; citing IS 2062:2011 as current (it was withdrawn after 29 Jun 2026; cite IS 2062 (Part 1):2025).
- Cite standards in the report by clause and table number with short paraphrase only. The numbers above come from free official previews (iTeh) and free primary PDFs (IACS Rec. 47, BIS texts on law.resource.org). Do not reproduce full tables beyond the few values used.

## Risks

- Depth mismatch: the standards grade by depth, and a single 2D camera measures only in-plane size. Severity can be wrong unless depth-governed findings default to HOLD for an operator check.
- Material mismatch: the owned coupons are aluminium. EN 10163, IS 2062 and ISO 8501 apply to steel, and aluminium forms no red rust or mill scale, so a rust demo on aluminium is not credible. UNVERIFIED whether the team has mild-steel coupons.
- Small-coupon distortion: the ISO 7788 / IACS affected-area method (20 mm margin, 40 mm clusters) makes a single small pit about 18 % of a 100 mm coupon (calc). Either state that raw defect-area fraction is used on coupons, or the area percentages will look absurd.
- Resolution limit: at 0.065 mm/px, a 0.127-0.2 mm scratch is only 2-3 px wide (calc). Fine scratches may be missed or flicker between frames, and specular glints on bright metal can create false scratches. Report the minimum detectable width that was actually measured.
- Lighting dependence: colour-based rust estimates and the visual grades themselves (IS 9954 compares against prints under defined light) depend on illumination and white balance. An unqualified 'irrespective of lighting' claim can be disproved at the demo.
