# Mechanical data for the Raspberry Pi AI Camera (IMX500), Pi 5 FPC cables, and 5-inch display options (Touch Display 2 5", Waveshare 5" DSI 800x480/(C)/(D), Waveshare 5" HDMI (H)), with housing-mounting rules for a Fusion 360 FDM handheld

(Research for the handheld enclosure, 5 Nov 2026. Every number carries its source; UNVERIFIED and (calc) are marked.)

## Summary

AI CAMERA. The official product brief (https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/camera/ai-camera-product-brief.pdf, p.4) has a dimensioned drawing, labelled 'approximate'. I rendered it at 400-800 dpi and read it off.
- PCB: 25.0 x 23.862 mm. Four holes of dia 2.2 mm (M2 clearance) with dia 4.75 pads. Hole pattern is 21.0 x 12.5 mm, with the hole rows 2.0 mm and 14.5 mm from the edge opposite the FPC connector.
- Lens axis: board centre in X (12.5 mm), 14.4 mm from that same edge (9.46 mm from the connector edge, calc).
- Lens barrel dia 5.75 mm. Holder footprint 12.0 x 12.5 mm, 3.875 mm tall. Behind it is a 12.8 x 19.5 x 1.0 mm sensor plate, then a 1.12 mm main PCB. The 15-pin connector is on the back, 19.61 mm wide, 5.71 mm deep from the edge, standing 2.75 mm proud. Overall depth is 11.9 mm (spec).
- The PCB outline and holes are identical to Camera Module 3, so the official CM3 STEP can be the base model: https://pip-assets.raspberrypi.com/categories/1207-design-files/documents/RP-008154-DS-1-camera-module-3-step.zip. No AI Camera STEP exists on the product information portal (pip.raspberrypi.com).
- Field of view: H 66.3 +/-3 deg (along the 25 mm board width), V 52.3 +/-3 deg (brief), diagonal 78.3 deg (Waveshare). Focus is manual, 20 cm to infinity, set with the included lens tool.
- At 200 mm the field is about 261 x 196 mm (calc). A window 2 mm in front of the lens needs an opening of about 7.5 x 6.4 mm (dia 8.5 mm) (calc, with margin).

CABLES. The official Pi 5 cables are Standard-Mini (22-way 0.5 mm to 15-way 1 mm) in 200, 300 and 500 mm lengths. Camera cables and display cables are separate products and are not interchangeable. The official standard cable drawing gives 16.0 mm width, 0.055 mm insulation, 0.027 mm conductor, and a 6.3 mm stiffener at a 0.309 mm contact thickness. Raspberry Pi gives no bend radius, only 'do not fold or strain'. The usual flex rule is 6-10x thickness; use R >= 3 mm in the housing.

DISPLAYS.
(a) Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2, 5-inch, EXISTS (SC1975). Launched Aug 2025 at $40; product brief published Oct 2026.
- 720x1280 portrait panel, 500 cd/m2, anti-glare, 1.4 W.
- Glass 91.46 x 143.4 mm, R5 corners, 0.7 mm cover glass. Active area 62.1 x 110.4 mm, visible area 63.1 x 111.4 mm.
- Depth is 14.92 mm from the glass front to the tips of four M2.5 Pi standoffs. The standoffs sit in the Pi's 49 x 58 mm hole pattern, so a Pi 5 bolts straight onto the display back.
- Four more bracket holes on a 51.0 x 103.7 mm pattern.
- Connects by DSI with the supplied Standard-Mini FFC, and takes 5 V from GPIO pins 2 and 6 through J1.
(b) Waveshare 5inch DSI LCD (800x480 TFT): glass 120.7 x 77.2 mm, PCB 121 x 76 mm, 8x M2.5 holes (113 x 68 mm plus the 58 x 49 mm Pi pattern), 13.0 mm thick. It is the easiest to buy in India (about Rs 3,400-3,800).
  - Variant (C), 1024x600 IPS: same glass and PCB, 13.4 mm thick, STEP file available.
  - Variant (D), 720x1280 IPS with optical bonding: glass 70.7 x 126.9 mm, 13.1 mm thick including 8 mm Pi standoffs, powered by pogo pins.
(c) Waveshare 5inch HDMI LCD (H), 800x480: outline 121 x 89.48 mm (V4). It needs a micro-HDMI cable or adapter, a USB touch cable and optionally separate power, which is the worst packaging for a handheld.

RECOMMENDATION:
- First choice is the official Touch Display 2 5".
  - Pros: documented drawing, Pi mounts on it, in production until Jan 2030, lowest power.
  - Weak point: availability in India. Rees52 lists Rs 4,235 but it is sold out.
- Fallback is the Waveshare 5inch DSI LCD (800x480, or (C) 1024x600), which is in stock at Robocraze, Zbotic and Robu.
- Avoid HDMI.

## Design rules

- In Fusion 360, build the camera from the official Camera Module 3 STEP (RP-008154 zip, same PCB, holes and connector). Then replace its lens stack with the AI Camera stack:
- holder 12.0 x 12.5 x 3.875 mm
- barrel dia 5.75, front 6.98 from the plate face
- sensor plate 12.8 x 19.5 x 1.0
- PCB 1.12
- connector 19.61 x 5.71 x 2.75 on the back
Put the key values in User Parameters (lens_axis_from_conn_edge = 9.46, hole_pitch_x = 21.0, hole_pitch_y = 12.5). Caliper-check the owned unit before printing the final part.
- Camera mounting: 4 bosses on the 21.0 x 12.5 mm pattern, boss OD <= 4.5 mm so they sit inside the dia 4.75 pads. Fasten with M2 x 5-6 self-tapping plastic screws into dia 1.6-1.7 pilot holes, or with M2 heat-set inserts (hole size per insert brand, est. dia 3.2). Keep at least 0.5 mm clearance around the 12.8 x 19.5 sensor plate and the 12 x 12.5 holder.
- Camera window: place the lens front 0-2 mm behind the outer face of the front wall. With d = 2 mm, cut at least a 7.5 (H) x 6.4 (V) mm opening or a dia 8.5 mm round hole (calc). Chamfer the wall outward at about 45 deg so the wall edge never enters the 66 x 52 deg (+3 deg) cone. Orient the long side of the opening along the camera's 25 mm board width (HFOV axis).
- If a protective window sits in front of the lens:
- Keep the window within 1 mm of the lens.
- Use thin AR glass or clear PC.
- Add a matte-black gasket/hood between window and lens, so light from the 12 LED strips cannot pipe through the window edge into the lens.
- The window's clear aperture must follow the d-table (d = 5 mm needs about 12 x 10 mm, dia 14).
- Print the lens surround and hood in matte black. Keep every LED strip behind the lens-front plane or behind a baffle, so no LED is visible from the lens (checked against the 42.65 deg diagonal half-angle).
- Focus: set focus at the working distance (about 200 mm) with the supplied lens tool BEFORE final closure. Make the lens hood/bezel a separate screw-on or clip-on part so the tool can reach the barrel later. Do not rely on the lens holding focus past its 20 cm limit (UNVERIFIED).
- Camera FPC: leave at least 8 mm free space beyond the connector edge of the camera PCB (that edge is 9.46 mm from the lens axis) for straight exit. Allow at least 3.5 mm behind the PCB for the 2.75 mm connector plus latch.
- Route all FFCs in printed channels about 1 mm deeper than the cable and 1 mm wider (17-18 mm for the 16 mm standard cable). Use bend formers with R >= 3 mm. Never crease within the stiffened 6.3 mm ends. Secure flat runs with Kapton tape, not by clamping.
- Pi 5 has two 22-way CAM/DISP ports:
- Put the DSI display on one; the TD2 5" overlay defaults to DSI-1, append ,dsi0 for port 0.
- Put the AI Camera on the other.
- Use a Standard-Mini CAMERA cable for the camera and a Standard-Mini DISPLAY cable for the display. They are not interchangeable.
- Order 200 mm cables, or 300 mm if the camera sits more than about 120 mm of cable path from the Pi (est.).
- TD2 5" layout: use it in landscape (143.4 wide x 91.46 tall), rotated in software (Screens > Orientation, or touch swapxy/invx/invy overlay params). Mount the Pi 5 on the display's four M2.5 standoffs, 49 x 58 mm pattern, 8.5 mm tall. - TD2 power: the J1 lead normally plugs onto GPIO pins 2 (5 V) and 6 (GND). If the AI HAT+ 2 stack covers those pins, either plug onto the HAT's pass-through header pins or feed J1 from the 5 V distribution rail sharing Pi ground (about 0.3 A, est. from the 1.4 W rating). Do not use pin 6 for the 12 V LED ground.

Thicker overlays degrade projected-capacitive touch (est., no vendor number found). Do not use HDMI panels in the handheld: the micro-HDMI cable, adapter and USB-touch cable bends need far more depth (est. 30-45 mm) than one DSI FFC.

## Risks

- The AI Camera drawing is labelled 'approximate' and reuses Camera Module 3 depth values. The reference face for 6.98 mm is ambiguous by about 0.4 mm, so caliper the real unit before fixing the lens-to-window distance.
- Lens focus closer than 20 cm, and the lens tool's size/engagement, are UNVERIFIED. A printed bezel that covers the barrel can block refocusing.
- The LED strips flanking the lens can cause flare or veiling glare through an unbaffled window. A window more than 2 mm from the lens grows quickly: d = 10 mm needs a dia 23 mm opening.
- Camera vs display FFC mix-up: Standard-Mini camera and display cables are wired differently. The wrong one gives no image (possibly harmful, UNVERIFIED).
- TD2 5" availability in India is weak (Rees52 sold out, no Robu/Robocraze/Probots listing found), so lead time is a risk. The Waveshare 800x480 DSI is the in-stock fallback but has a lower-resolution TFT panel.
- The TD2's 0.7 mm cover glass overhangs the module by 9.25 mm. Clamping or drop impact on that overhang can crack it; the brief warns the display 'has the potential to shatter'.
- TD2 GPIO power (pins 2 and 6) may conflict with the AI HAT+ 2 occupying the header. Whether the HAT's stacking header leaves pins exposed is for the Pi/HAT research.
- TD2 thickness: the labelled 14.92 mm minus 8.5 mm gives 6.42 mm to the back plane, but the scaled drawing suggests about 7.3 mm. Bosses designed exactly to 6.42 mm may bottom out.
- The Waveshare (D) pogo-pin power only works with the Pi mounted directly on it. Any other Pi placement needs the PH2.0 5 V lead.
- FDM tolerance and warping on a large (about 150 mm) bezel can bow the glass pocket. Split the bezel, add ribs, or print in PETG/ABS. Allow 0.25 mm per side clearance and verify with a test-print of the pocket corner.
- Retailer INR prices are snapshots and some conflict (Zbotic shows two prices for the (D)). Re-check before ordering.
