# Ergonomics and FDM printability of an F1-steering-wheel-style two-hand handheld enclosure (Pi 5 + AI HAT+ 2 + AI Camera + 5-inch display + power bank + 12 LED strips) for Fusion 360 CAD

(Research for the handheld enclosure, 5 Nov 2026. Every number carries its source; UNVERIFIED and (calc) are marked.)

## Summary

Real F1 wheels and their replicas measure about 280-290 mm grip to grip and weigh 0.9-1.3 kg. Grips are up to 43 mm thick. The closest handheld-with-screen benchmark is the Steam Deck: 298 x 117 x 49 mm, 640-669 g.

Recommended envelope for this device (calc/est.):
- Overall width 280-290 mm. Central body 165-170 W x 115-125 H x 60-70 D mm.
- Grip centre-to-centre about 240 mm. Grips are 110-120 mm long, oval about 36 x 32 mm including a 1.5-2 mm TPU sleeve.
- Total mass at or below 1.0 kg. A realistic build lands at about 0.95-1.05 kg (calc): the 251 g power bank and about 280 g of PETG shell dominate.

Indian anthropometry sets the grip length. North-Indian male hand breadth is 85.0 +/- 5.7 mm, so the P95 is about 94.4 mm (calc). A BIS draft gives P95 89 mm at the metacarpals.

Thumbs cannot reach the centre of a landscape 5-inch screen (Touch Display 2 is 143.5 x 91.5 x 16 mm, active area 110.5 x 62 mm). Put on-screen controls at the outer edges, physical buttons between the screen and the grips, and a capture trigger under each index finger on the front face (the F1-paddle analogue).

No printer in the list fits the wheel in one piece except the H2D and A2L. A 290 x 120 mm part does not fit a 256 mm bed even diagonally (calc). So split it into a central body of 170 mm or less plus two grip/arm pieces of 110 mm or less. Join them with tongue-and-groove, steel dowels and M3 bolts into heat-set inserts. Every part then stays at or below 210 x 210 mm, and below 170 mm for the A1 mini.

Material: use PETG, not PLA. Prusament PETG HDT is 68 °C and PLA HDT is 55 °C. The AI HAT+ 2 is rated for 0-50 °C ambient, and Bengaluru reached 38.5 °C in April 2024. ASA is optional for the front light plate if an enclosed printer is available. Use black material for every part near the camera and the UV LEDs, because white or natural plastics with optical brighteners fluoresce at 400-500 nm.

Heat-set inserts (CNC Kitchen and ruthex tables):
- M2: hole Ø3.2, minimum wall 1.3, so a boss OD of about 6.0.
- M2.5 and M3: hole Ø4.0, minimum wall 1.6, so a boss OD of 7.5-8.0.
- Blind-hole depth is the insert length + 1 mm. Use straight holes. Iron temperature for PETG is about 245 °C.

Vents:
- Inlet slots on the bottom face of the central body and outlet slots on the top face. Never on the grips or the display face.
- Slots 2.0 mm wide (below the 2.5 mm IP3X probe) with 1.6-2.0 mm ribs.
- At least 600 mm² free area each side, 1000 mm² preferred, to keep the 1.09 CFM Active Cooler flow below about 0.5-1 m/s.
- Thermal margin is tight (calc). 13-15 W heats even fully fresh Active-Cooler air by about 22 K. Plan an internal intake/exhaust partition and space for an optional 30 mm fan.

Lighting:
- The camera FOV is H 66° and V 52.3° (+/-3°), which gives 260 x 196 mm at 200 mm (calc). Size the hood walls to 36° and 29° half-angles.
- The hood rim must sit at least 3 mm proud of every LED or diffuser exit face. Put an opaque black wall of at least 1.6 mm between the camera and every LED pocket, and never use one shared window.
- Toe-in: atan(d/200). That is 16.7° at 60 mm offset, 21.8° at 80 mm and 26.6° at 100 mm. It is essential for the narrow-beam 5 mm UV LEDs (typically 30° full angle). It is optional for the 120° white modules, where it gains 8% or less.
- Every LED within ±130 mm of the lens will leave a specular hotspot on flat shiny metal at 200 mm (calc). Diffusers and multi-light capture are the mitigation.

- **Vent openings vs IEC 60529**, Probe sizes: IP2X: 12.5 sphere / 12 mm jointed finger, 80 long. IP3X: 2.5 probe must not enter. IP4X: 1.0 wire. A 2.0 mm slot width keeps out 2.5 mm tools and fingers. (high; https://www.orientalmotor.com/technology/ip-ratings-chart.html ; https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/ip-ingress-protection-d_452.html)
- **Vent sizing (calc)**, Free area and thermal budget: Active Cooler 1.09 CFM = 0.000514 m³/s. Free area for 1 m/s = 514 mm², for 0.5 m/s = 1029 mm², so specify >= 600 mm² inlet + >= 600 mm² outlet (1000 preferred), e.g. 15 slots of 2.0 x 20 = 600 mm². Air temperature rise for 13 W through 1.09 CFM ≈ 21.6 K. Keeping a 15 W load at ΔT ≤ 15 K needs ≈ 1.81 CFM (ρ·cp ≈ 1168 J/m³K). (medium; calc from https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/active-cooler/)
- **Light leakage reference**, Gasket rule under a cover window: An opaque gasket is needed when the air gap under a shared cover exceeds ~0.5 mm (ST forum, ToF sensors). Shared flash/camera windows need opaque perimeter grooves (patent). (medium; https://community.st.com/t5/imaging-sensors/vl53l5cx-gasket-requirements-with-cover-window-and-large-air-gap/m-p/142461 ; https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12425706)
- **UV transmission / fluorescence**, Cut-off and fluorescence bands: Optical brighteners absorb 340-400 nm and re-emit 400-500 nm (white/natural plastics glow blue under 395 nm). Standard UV-absorber acrylic blocks to ~360 nm; some framing grades block <400 nm; most acrylic passes >375 nm. Neat PETG blocks only ≤300 nm, but PETG with UV absorber blocks to ~400 nm. Polycarbonate cut-off is UNVERIFIED. (medium; https://www.photonics.com/Article.aspx?AID=16633 ; https://simplyplastics.com/ideas-and-advice/the-truth-about-perspex-uv-resistance-and-why-its-important ; https://bioresources.cnr.ncsu.edu/?p=44617)

## Design rules

- FUSION 360 SETUP: define every number below as a User Parameter (Modify > Change Parameters). Examples: clr_slip=0.2, clr_pocket=0.4, wall=2.2, ins_M3_d=4.0, ins_M3_depth=6.7, toe_in=atan(d_led/200). Use Split Body on the parametric master body to cut the build-plate parts, so a resize propagates.
- OVERALL GEOMETRY (calc/est.): overall width 280-290. Central body 165-170 W x 115-125 H x 60-70 D. Grip c-c 240-245. If the touchscreen must be thumb-usable, shrink grip c-c to 215-225 (overall ~255-260), like the Steam Deck at 298 x 117 x 49.
- GRIPS: straight length 110-120 (>= P95 Indian male breadth 94.4 + margin). Oval 36 (front-back) x 32 (side) outside the TPU. Rigid PETG core 32 x 28 + 1.5-2.0 TPU 95A sleeve. Grip ends R >= 8. Hand-contact edges R3-R5. Splay 10-15° and rake 15-20° bottom toward user (est.): prototype in PLA and try it in the hand.
- CONTROLS: thumbs reach only the outer 0-25 mm of the 110.5 mm wide active area. Put on-screen soft keys in the outer 20 mm of the screen. Put 2-3 tactile buttons per side on the rear face between display edge and grip, centred 30-45 mm inward of the grip axis. Put the capture trigger under the index finger on the front face of each grip (F1-paddle analogue), outside the LED beams and camera FOV.
- MASS AND CoM: target ≤ 1.0 kg total (Steam Deck 640-669 g; F-CORE 895 g; CCOHS 1.4 kg one-hand limit). Place the power bank (~251 g, the heaviest part) centred left-right, low in the body, at the same depth as the grip axes. Keep the CoM within ~±10 mm of the line joining the grip centres (est.).
- WALLS: outer shell 2.0-2.4 (≈4-5 perimeters at 0.45). Internal ribs/partitions 1.6. Light baffles ≥1.6 in black. Nothing below 1.2 (min 2 perimeters, Prusa table). Set the slicer to 4-5 walls rather than relying on infill.
- OVERHANGS/BRIDGES: ≤45° unsupported (≤60° on Bambu/Prusa with good cooling). Bridges ≤5 mm cosmetic, ≤10 mm structural. Hollow grip interiors get a teardrop/45° pointed roof, or print grips solid with 15-20% gyroid (≈60 g per grip, calc).
- EDGES: 0.4-0.6 x 45° chamfer on every bed-contact edge (elephant foot). Chamfer rather than fillet on bed-facing edges. Internal wall-boss junctions R1 + 1.2-1.6 gussets.
- CLEARANCES: PCB/display/power-bank pockets +0.4 per side (+0.3 min). Removable lids/slides 0.15-0.20 per side. Hinges/moving ≥0.3. Alignment pegs 0.10-0.15. Press fit 0-0.05. Snap fits 0.5 nominal. Print a fit-test coupon first: printed holes come out undersized, so add +0.1-0.2 to vertical holes (est.) and use teardrop horizontal holes.
- HEAT-SET INSERTS (CNC Kitchen / ruthex): M2 → Ø3.2 hole, depth L + 1 (4.0 for M2x3, 5.0 for M2x4), boss OD 6.0. M2.5 → Ø4.0 hole, depth 5.0 (CNC K M2.5x4) or 6.7 (ruthex M2.5x5.7), boss OD 7.5-8.0. M3 → Ø4.0 hole, depth 6.7 (M3x5.7) or 4.0 (M3x3 short), boss OD 7.5-8.0. Straight holes, no taper, axis along Z, installed from the open side. Iron ~245 °C for PETG.
- FASTENER ALLOCATION: M3 x 5.7 inserts for shell-to-shell joints and lids. M2.5 inserts or M2.5 brass standoffs for the Pi 5 (Ø2.7 holes, 58 x 49 pattern, 3.5 from edges, step boss Ø7.5 → Ø5.5 top land ≤ Ø6 pad) and display (M2.5 corner standoffs). M2 for the camera (footprint = Camera Module 3; hole pattern UNVERIFIED, measure). Clearance holes M2 2.4, M2.5 2.9, M3 3.4 (ISO 273 medium).
- SELF-TAPPING only for parts opened <5 times (est.): pilot d − 0.4 (M3 2.6, M2.5 2.1, M2 1.6). Boss OD 2·d (6 for M3). Engagement ≥ 2·d. Lay bosses on the bed when possible.
- SNAP FITS only for small covers (battery/connector doors). PETG, never PLA. Cantilever in the XY plane. Root fillet ≥0.5·t, width ≥5, tapered. y = 0.67·ε·L²/t with ε ≤ 2%; e.g. t 1.6, L 12 → undercut 0.8-1.0. The main shell closes with screws.
- PART SPLIT (≤210 x 210 per part; ≤170 for A1 mini): (1) rear centre shell (display side, ≤170 x 125); (2) front centre shell; (3) left grip/arm; (4) right grip/arm, each ≤110 x 130; (5) front light plate in black PETG/ASA with LED pockets, camera hood and diffuser windows, so the optics can be re-tuned without reprinting the shell; (6) swappable display bezel (TD2 5-inch 143.5 x 91.5 x 16 or Waveshare); (7) internal Pi/HAT tray; (8) 2 TPU sleeves. Joints sit outside the display width (x = ±85).
- SPLIT JOINTS: lap/overlap ≥15 (est.). Tongue 2.0 W x 3.0 H into groove 2.4 W x 3.5 D (0.2 per side, 0.5 bottom gap). 2 x Ø3 x 10 steel dowels per joint, Ø3.0 press on one side and Ø3.2 slip on the other, each ≥2·d = 6 deep, with a small glue gutter. Plus 2 x M3 screws into M3x5.7 inserts. Optional CA/epoxy for a permanent build.
- PRINT ORIENTATION: front centre shell front-face-down (camera/LED openings become vertical through-holes; toe-in LED seats are up-facing slopes, no support). Rear shell display-face-down. Grip pieces lie flat on the split plane. Insert bosses along Z. Snap-fit beams and thin clips in XY. Use a 5 mm brim on large PETG/ASA parts.
- MATERIALS: all structural and near-heat parts in PETG (HDT 68-69 °C). PLA only for fit-check prototypes (HDT 55 °C; ambient up to 38.5 °C plus self-heating; creeps at inserts). ASA for the front light plate only on an enclosed printer (P1S/X1C/K1/Core One); it softens at 110 °C, is UV-stable, but has weaker layers (17 kg vs 25 kg PETG). Black filament for the light plate, hood, baffles and any part seeing the UV LEDs. Never white/natural near UV (brightener fluorescence 400-500 nm).
- TPU GRIPS: 95A, 1.5-2.0 thick sleeves printed separately and slid on, with 0-0.2 interference and 1 mm ridges keying into grooves in the PETG core. If co-printed, use interlocking geometry, 105-110% flow and fan off for 2-3 interface layers. Feed 95A from an external spool, not the AMS.
- VENTS: inlets on the bottom face of the central body, outlets on the top face (stack effect + blower exhaust). None on the grips, the display face, or within ~25 mm of where palms and fingers wrap (est.). Slots 2.0 W x 15-25 L, ribs 1.6-2.0, oriented so the slot roof is ≤2 mm bridge. Free area ≥600 mm² each (≥1000 preferred). Add an internal partition so blower exhaust cannot recirculate to the intake gap between Pi and HAT (16 mm header). Keep the 5→12 V LED boost converter and LED pockets out of the intake path. Leave a 30 x 30 x 7 mm pocket and 2-pin header access for an optional 5 V exhaust fan near the HAT heatsink. Optional dust filter is open-cell foam or fine mesh on the inlet; re-check temperatures with it fitted.
- CAMERA: centre the camera on the device's vertical axis, directly behind the display centre, so the screen view matches the aim. Pocket 25.6 x 24.6 (module + 0.3/side) x ≥12.5 deep. Hood = rectangular frustum 8-10 deep from the lens front, wall half-angles 36° H / 29° V, matte black, removable (2 x M2) so the AI Camera focus ring stays reachable. AI Camera focus is specified from 20 cm, so set it at ~200 mm.
- LIGHT LEAKAGE: no shared transparent window across camera and LEDs; each LED group gets its own window. A continuous black wall ≥1.6 between camera pocket and every LED pocket, running to the front face. Hood rim ≥3 mm proud of every LED/diffuser exit face, so no emitting surface has line of sight to the lens. LED emitting faces recessed 3-5 behind the front face. Any cover over the camera gets an opaque foam gasket (air gap >0.5 mm needs one).
- TOE-IN: angle each LED seat at atan(d/200), with d = lateral offset from the lens axis. Use 11° at 40 mm, 17° at 60, 22° at 80, 27° at 100, 31° at 120. Mandatory for the 5 mm UV LEDs (≈30° beam, 107 mm spot at 200 mm). For the 120° white 2835 modules it is cosmetic (≤8-14% gain) but still worth modelling to keep spill off the hood.
- DIFFUSERS: white modules get a 2 mm opal acrylic window, or 0.8-1.2 natural PETG at 100% infill printed cool, mounted 6-10 in front of the LEDs (0.3-1.2 x pitch). UV LEDs get no diffuser or a UV-transmissive one (plain PMMA without UV absorber, or neat PETG). Avoid polycarbonate and UV-filtering acrylic until checked with the camera at 395 nm. Diffusers turn sharp specular hotspots into broad, lower-peak glare.
- GLARE: every LED on a ≤290 mm wheel lies within the ±260 mm H / ±196 mm V zone that hotspots on flat shiny parts at 200 mm. Mitigate with diffusers, sequential single-light captures (one strip group at a time) and a slight part tilt. Linear-polarizer film on LED windows plus a crossed polarizer on the lens is an optional low-cost add.
- LED THERMAL: 6 white modules = 7.2 W and 18 UV LEDs ≈ 1.2 W electrical (calc). Mount modules on 1 mm standoff ribs (air gap) or a 1 mm aluminium strip, never flat on PETG. Measure module back temperature: it must stay well below 68 °C (PETG HDT).

## Risks

- THERMAL: the AI HAT+ 2 is rated to 50 °C ambient, but Bengaluru reached 38.5 °C. That leaves ~11-15 K of internal rise for ~13-15 W of Pi 5 + Hailo + display. The Active Cooler (1.09 CFM) alone heats fresh air by ~22 K at 13 W (calc), so summer throttling (80/85 °C) is likely. Measure with vcgencmd and keep the optional fan pocket.
- PLA ENCLOSURE: HDT 55 °C means sagging or insert pull-out inside a closed case, in a parked car, or next to the 1.2 W LED modules. Do not ship PLA parts.
- SPECULAR GLARE: all LEDs within ±130 mm of the lens produce in-frame hotspots on flat shiny metal at 200 mm (calc). Defect models trained on diffuse images may misfire.
- LIGHT LEAKAGE AND FLUORESCENCE: thin or light-coloured PETG passes light, and white/natural PLA or PETG with brighteners glows blue under 395 nm, adding stray light and colour cast. Use black, ≥1.6 mm walls.
- THUMB REACH: with an F1-like grip spacing (~244 mm c-c) thumbs reach only the outer edge of the 5-inch landscape screen (est.). Touch UI in the centre forces a one-hand grip, which moves the CoM and shakes the camera.
- WEIGHT: the realistic build is ~0.95-1.05 kg (calc), ≈1.5x a Steam Deck, so arm fatigue sets in over minutes of aiming. Use a 10000 mAh bank (251 g), not a 20000 mAh (435 g), and 2.0 mm walls where drop risk allows.
- SPLIT JOINTS take the full grip load and drop impact. A joint inside a grip, or one without dowels and bolts, will crack along layers (PETG vertical layer strength is ~46% of flat).
- BUILD VOLUME: Bambu's 256 mm volume is not fully usable (forum: ~256 x 238 x 249 depending on orientation). A 290 mm wheel needs splitting on every printer except the H2D/A2L.
- UNKNOWN PART GEOMETRY: PickTheDeal module size, lens and beam, the team's UV LED viewing angle, the AI Camera lens barrel diameter and protrusion, the TD2 5-inch mounting-hole pattern, and the power bank model are all unconfirmed. Pockets and the hood must be finalised from calipers.
- AI CAMERA FOCUS: specified 20 cm to infinity. A 150 mm working distance may be outside the focus range (UNVERIFIED). Keep the hood removable for focus adjustment.
- TPU feeding through Bambu AMS is unreliable (forum). The TPU-PETG bond varies by brand, so mechanically key the sleeves.
- Heat-set boss Ø7.5 is larger than the Ø6 Pi 5 pad: an unstepped boss can touch nearby components. Use a stepped boss or brass standoffs.
