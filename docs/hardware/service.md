# Ayas handheld: modules, fasteners, wiring and repair (Phase 2)

How the handheld comes apart and goes back together. Electronics are in `docs/hardware/pcb_phase2.md` and the board files in `hardware/pcb/`; the housing is generated by `hardware/enclosure/ayas_handheld_fusion.py`.

Three rules hold the design together:

1. **Every service screw goes into a brass heat-set insert, never into bare plastic.** Each joint can be opened as often as needed.
2. **Every cable is keyed and has a different connector at each place, so no plug fits a wrong socket.** On LIGHT/IO the JST-SH sockets are all different pin counts. The USB-to-Pi and power-button cables use JST-PH, so they cannot meet an SH cable. `tests/test_hardware_design.py` checks this, and checks that both ends of every cable carry the same pinout.
3. **Loose leads go into push-in terminals, not solder.** That covers the white modules and the buttons. The pods and the hood connect without a cable at all: the pod on a flex into a ZIF, the hood on spring pins.

## 1. Modules

| Module | Comes off with | Connects by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LED pod L / R (identical parts) | 2 × M3 from the front (counterbored) | its flex into the POD DOCK ZIF in the body front face | the dock decides the side, so a pod fits either side |
| Inspection hood | 2 × M3 thumb screws | 9 gold pads on the HOOD DOCK spring pins | UV works only with the hood on (the interlock loop is in the hood pad board) |
| Camera hood | 2 × M2 | none | |
| Battery (power bank) | BayDoor, 4 × M3 | USB-C right-angle lead to POWER J1 | |
| Back lid with display, Pi 5, AI HAT+ 2 | M3 countersunk screws | display DSI ribbon, Pi USB to LIGHT/IO (JST-PH 4), Pi 5 V from POWER J3, USB-A export extension | |
| Wings L / R | M3 × 16 joining screws | SIDE board cable (JST-SH 4 or 5) to LIGHT/IO | |

## 2. Fasteners

| Where | Screw | Insert (ruthex or CNC Kitchen) | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| POWER and LIGHT/IO boards | M3 × 6 pan | M3 short, 5.7 mm | 8 |
| LED pods to the body | M3 × 12, counterbored | M3 × 6.7 | 4 |
| BayDoor, BackLid, wing joints, hood thumb screws | M3 (as in the generator) | M3 × 6.7 | per CAD |
| UV socket board in each pod | M2 × 5 | M2 × 4 | 4 |
| WHITE carrier, outer edge (the inner edge slides under a lip) | M2 × 5 | M2 × 4 | 4 |
| White module tabs (pods and hood ring) | M2 × 6 pan | pods: Würth WA-SMSI SMD nut on the carrier; hood ring: M2 × 4 insert | 24 |
| Camera board, camera hood | M2 × 5 | M2 × 4 | 6 |
| CHARGE board | M2 × 5 | M2 × 4 (in the wall rib) | 1 |
| POD DOCK, HOOD DOCK, HOOD PAD, SIDE boards | M2 × 5 | M2 × 4 | 2 each |

- **Kit:** one M2 and one M3 screw length cover most joints. A 1.5 mm and a 2 mm hex key (or PH0 / PH1) is the whole tool kit.

## 3. Cable table

Every connector's function is printed on the silkscreen next to it, on the board's back as well where the plug goes in from behind.

| Cable | From | To | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LINK | POWER J2 | LIGHT/IO J1 | JST-PH 12, straight | across the bay, along the shelf |
| Pi 5 V | POWER J3 | Pi 5 | as built | |
| Power button | POWER J5 | 12 mm ring-LED button, top | JST-PH 4 | |
| Charge port | CHARGE J2 | POWER J6 | JST-PH 5 | up through the shelf hole |
| USB to the Pi | LIGHT/IO J2 | Pi USB 2.0 port | JST-PH 4 to USB-A | RP2040 serial and the PI_ON sense |
| POD-L | LIGHT/IO J3 | POD DOCK L J2 | JST-SH 6 | |
| POD-R | LIGHT/IO J4 | POD DOCK R J2 | JST-SH 7 | |
| HOOD | LIGHT/IO J5 | HOOD DOCK J2 | JST-SH 9 | |
| SIDE-L | LIGHT/IO J6 | SIDE-L J4 (left wing) | JST-SH 4 | |
| SIDE-R | LIGHT/IO J7 | SIDE-R J4 (right wing) | JST-SH 5 | |
| FAN | LIGHT/IO J9 | 30 mm fan | JST-SH 2 | |
| Pod flex | pod hub J1 | POD DOCK J1 | flex, ZIF 10 | palindrome: either way round |
| White carrier | WHITE J4 | pod hub J2 | JST-PH 2, side entry | inside the pod |

- **Cable lengths** follow the routes above. Measure them on the first build and add them to this table; make each cable 10 mm longer than its route so connectors unplug without strain.
- **Routing.** Cables run along the shelf and the side walls, never over the Pi's heatsink or across the fan.

## 4. Assembly order

1. Press the heat-set inserts into all printed parts (soldering iron at 220 °C).
2. **FrontCentre.**
   - Fit the camera, the CHARGE board, both POD DOCKs and the HOOD DOCK.
   - Fit POWER and LIGHT/IO on the shelf bosses.
   - Plug in LINK, CHARGE, POD-L, POD-R and HOOD.
3. **Wings.** Fit the buttons, then the SIDE boards; push in the button leads (TRIG, UPPER, LOWER); join the wings and plug SIDE-L and SIDE-R into LIGHT/IO.
4. **Pods** (on the bench).
   - Fit the UV socket board, the WHITE carrier and the 3 white modules.
   - Push in the UV LEDs (long leg to +).
   - Plug the carrier into the hub and the flex into the hub ZIF.
   - Then: flex into the body's dock ZIF, and 2 × M3.
5. **Back lid.**
   - Plug in USB, Pi 5 V and the display ribbon.
   - Close the lid.
   - Fit the bank and the BayDoor.
6. **Hood.** Thumb screws on. Check: UV is offered on screen only with the hood on.

## 5. Repairs

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| A UV LED dead | Pull it out from the pod front, push a new one in (long leg to the "+" socket). Nothing opened. |
| A white module dead (pod) | Pod off (2 × M3) → module tab screws (2 × M2) → press the 2 terminal buttons → swap → push the leads in (red to +). |
| A hood ring module dead | Same, inside the hood: tab screws into inserts, leads into the hood pad terminal R1–R6 marked for that sector. |
| A button dead | Wing back off → unscrew the button nut → press its 2 terminal buttons on the SIDE board → swap. |
| A pod damaged | Swap the whole pod: either pod fits either side. |
| No light at all | LIGHT/IO: check LINK and the INA226 reading in the service screen. Swap the board (4 × M3, 7 plugs, all different). |
| Will not power on | POWER: check the bank lead and the button cable (JST-PH 4). Swap the board (4 × M3, 5 plugs). |
| Will not charge | CHARGE board (1 × M2, 1 plug) or POWER. |

## 6. Verify on the first build

- **White module** (`WHITE_MOD` in design.py, `white_*` in the enclosure generator), measured with calipers:
  - tab-hole pitch (46 mm assumed);
  - height (6 mm);
  - LED pitch (12.5 mm).
- **Module current at 12 V**, to set the white and ring set-points in the firmware.
- **Spring pins** (Mill-Max 0906 series): stroke and force against the hood pad when the thumb screws are tight.
- **Cable lengths**, to fill in §3.
